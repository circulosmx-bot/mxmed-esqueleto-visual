## Timed urine, lithiasis and result boundary

Mayo's [SUP24 profile](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/616180) is a **true one-ID 24-hour panel**. Its profile table explicitly lists independently available sodium, potassium, calcium, magnesium, chloride, phosphorus, sulfate, citrate, oxalate, urine pH, uric acid, creatinine, osmolality and ammonium; the supersaturation calculations are not independently orderable. [Mayo's 24-hour urine protein test](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/614002) is likewise a real order. The random [stone-risk profile](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/616375) is a different source definition and must not be silently merged with SUP24 under one ambiguous key. Proposed `urine_stone_supersaturation_spot` and `urine_stone_supersaturation_24h` are therefore separate *source-profile* identities, not arbitrary duration variants of one analyte. Both are in the extended set pending versioned panel composition and provider mapping.

For individual analytes, one stable analyte identity plus `URINE` and requested `TIMED:1440` is preferred. Do not create `*_24h` keys for calcium, uric acid, citrate, oxalate, protein, magnesium, phosphate, chloride, sodium, potassium, sulfate, ammonium, creatinine or pH. The last three existing spot keys (`urine_creatinine_spot`, `urine_sodium_spot`, `urine_potassium_spot`) require a **future rule/identity review** before accepting timed collection; do not seed duplicate timed keys. The `urine_ph_standalone` proposal covers the source-supported isolated urine pH order; EGO's pH remains a result component. Urine volume, crystal saturation indices, estimated protein catabolic rate, Light's criteria and SAAG are collection facts or derived results, not independent physician orderables.

Mayo's 24-hour tests require the **actual measured volume and duration** at the performing lab. CAT03B carries only the *requested* duration. The physician may safely ask for an order and MXMED can retain the returned report, but MXMED must not calculate or assert 24-hour excretion, clearance or supersaturation from the request alone. [Mayo SUP24](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/616180) also specifies provider collection/preservative instructions and additional information for pediatric patients. This excludes timed urine orders and stone profiles from the 17-key minimum despite their source-supported clinical identity.

Stone decision: **14** timed urine analyte concepts, including 24-hour protein, are source-supported across the timed/stone rows; **2** true stone source profiles are proposed; calcium oxalate, calcium phosphate and uric-acid supersaturation are **3 derived outputs**. No standalone Light-criteria or SAAG study is proposed.

## Body-fluid identity and specimen rules

Recommend **strategy B with a selective existing-identity exception**: one explicit canonical identity per analyte or genuine profile, plus a controlled allowed-fluid list. Keep existing dedicated `csf_glucose` and `csf_total_protein`; CSF is excluded from generic body-fluid chemistry. No analyte × fluid key explosion. This follows source ordering patterns in [Mayo body-fluid cell count](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/608873), [body-fluid glucose](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/801559), [total protein](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/800538), [albumin](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/800507), [LDH](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/606612), [amylase](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/606595), [triglycerides](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/800539), and [cholesterol](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/606915). Each test has its own allowed-source set; a generic key is not permission to submit every body fluid.

`body_fluid_glucose` is safe with a mandatory choice among `PLEURAL_FLUID`, `ASCITIC_PERITONEAL_FLUID`, `SYNOVIAL_FLUID` and `PERICARDIAL_FLUID`; the source specifically routes CSF to its dedicated glucose order. Common cell count and total protein use these four types. Albumin, amylase, triglycerides and cholesterol use the three serous fluids in this proposal. LDH is restricted to pleural, peritoneal and synovial fluid; [Mayo cautions](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/606612) against assuming pericardial LDH has the same diagnostic utility. The matrix records each fluid row and the shared key. The optional [body-fluid lipid analysis panel](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/614164) is a true profile, distinct from triglycerides alone, and remains extended because source collection date/time and local offering need review.

| Fluid | Safe source-aware first-set orderables | Deferred/conditional |
|---|---|---|
| Pleural | cell count/differential; glucose, protein, albumin, LDH, amylase, triglycerides, cholesterol; shared bacterial culture; serous cytology | pH blood-gas collection path, isolated Gram, AFB, lipid panel |
| Ascitic/peritoneal | same chemistry/cell count, shared culture and cytology | standalone Gram, lipid profile; SAAG is derived |
| Synovial | cell count/differential, glucose, protein, LDH, shared culture; existing `synovial_crystals` | standalone Gram, cytology identity, lactate/uric acid evidence; urate and CPPD are crystal findings |
| Pericardial | cell count/differential, glucose, protein, albumin, amylase, triglycerides, cholesterol, shared culture and cytology | LDH uncertain clinical utility; standalone Gram and specialty analyses |

Pleural pH must follow a blood-gas style handling path: [Mayo's generic body-fluid pH](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/606522) expressly excludes pleural pH. A source choice alone does not solve that provider workflow.

## CSF, molecular, pathology and microbiology

- **Paired CSF/serum:** [Mayo oligoclonal bands](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/8017) and [IgG-index profile](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/610783) are single orderables requiring both CSF and serum. CAT03B's `SERUM:SAME_COLLECTION_EPISODE` is a deliberately stricter physician request than the sources' permissible collection window; the performing provider must enforce its actual acceptance window. Oligoclonal bands are in the first set; IgG index is extended because the source profile has additional aliquot/logistics requirements. CSF IgG, albumin, quotient and calculated index are their result components/calculations, not four more CTAs.
- **CSF molecular:** [Mayo's meningitis/encephalitis multiplex PCR](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/67846) is one orderable with fixed CSF; organism targets are results. An exact-order-item-linked PDF/unstructured document is sufficient for V1; structured organism-level results are a later enhancement. The source excludes shunt-derived CSF, which belongs in provider instructions. The primary navigation remains Molecular/Infectious; fluid navigation may be secondary.
- **CSF other:** lactate, cryptococcal antigen and VDRL have distinct source orders; CSF cytology is a distinct pathology request. CSF bacterial culture and fungal/AFB studies merit later microbiology review because their preanalytics and method/reflex semantics vary. CSF flow cytometry and serous-fluid flow are source-supported but require clinical history, CSF count and provider triage beyond specimen V1; defer one shared flow identity rather than inventing CSF/body-fluid duplicates. [Mayo flow definition](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/3287).
- **Cytology:** [Michigan's urine cytology](https://mlabs.umich.edu/tests/urine-bladder-ureteral-urethral-renal-pelvic-cytology) is a distinct common order; it is neither cervical cytology nor urine EGO. Its primary path is **Patología y biopsias**, with optional urine discovery. Dedicated [CSF cytology](https://mlabs.umich.edu/tests/cerebrospinal-fluid-cytology) and one source-aware serous-fluid cytology identity are preferable to separate pleural/ascitic/pericardial keys. An interpretive pathology report can be stored unstructured and linked to the order item; structured cytomorphology remains future work. Synovial cytology and flow remain deferred.
- **Sterile-fluid microbiology:** [Michigan's aerobic body-fluid culture](https://mlabs.umich.edu/tests/aerobic-culture-body-fluid) includes a Gram smear when specimen permits and susceptibility as downstream/reflex work. The first set therefore has **one** `sterile_body_fluid_bacterial_culture` with four allowed sterile fluids; it does not add cavity-specific culture keys, duplicate Gram CTAs, or a standalone susceptibility order. [Gram-only](https://mlabs.umich.edu/tests/gram-stain-smear) is a different order *only when culture is not requested* and needs mutual-exclusion/provider workflow before activation. [Fungal culture](https://mlabs.umich.edu/tests/fungus-culture-body-fluid) is source-supported and extended. AFB needs its own verified method/source routing. Existing `urine_culture` and `blood_culture` keep their distinct authority.
- **Other specimens:** a single source-aware respiratory aerobic culture is plausible for sputum/tracheal aspirate, with primary Microbiology navigation; BAL quantitative culture and bronchial-washing method are separately deferred pending exact provider mapping. Salivary cortisol is a real order but collection clock time is clinically required and outside V1; the vague “saliva analysis/culture” row is not a canonical identity. Wound, drain and amniotic specimens are absent from the V1 controlled vocabulary. Semen concentration/motility/morphology/volume/vitality/leukocytes remain result components of `semen_analysis`; strict morphology, antisperm testing and semen culture need exact offering review. The CAT02 source does not establish a separate sperm-DNA-fragmentation order, so the new gap row is explicitly uncertain.

The existing result model's exact order version and `related_order_item_ids` support a single interpretive PDF, multiple findings in a panel PDF, and precise provenance. This does **not** turn individual organism targets, pathologist observations, crystal species, or calculated gradients into separate order identities. Current routing groups suffice for all proposed safe keys: `CLINICAL_LAB`, `PATHOLOGY_CYTOLOGY`, or `GENETICS_MOLECULAR`; unresolved safe routing count is **0**. Navigation and routing remain distinct authorities.
