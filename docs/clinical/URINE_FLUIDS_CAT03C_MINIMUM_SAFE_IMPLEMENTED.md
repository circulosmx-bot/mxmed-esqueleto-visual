## Order and navigation behavior

The eight generic body-fluid analytes have one key each and explicit per-analyte allowed specimens. CSF glucose/protein/cell count keep their existing dedicated keys. The new CSF orders have fixed CSF; oligoclonal bands additionally snapshot required serum from the same collection episode through specimen contract V1. This is a physician request constraint; the performing provider still applies its collection acceptance window.

The full fluid catalog retains six featured studies and adds **Líquidos serosos** between LCR and Líquido sinovial: Estudios generales y renales → Electrolitos y minerales urinarios → Microbiología urinaria → Semen → LCR → Líquidos serosos → Líquido sinovial. HIER03 places culture and CSF infectious tests under Microbiología, the CSF multiplex panel under Molecular e infecciosas, and all three cytologies under Patología y biopsias. The fluid catalog exposes eight serous chemistry/cell-count identities, two additional CSF laboratory identities, and four approved generic fluid studies as secondary discovery within Líquido sinovial. There are no duplicate rows within a leaf.

The inline composer requires a controlled specimen choice for the nine configurable identities and shows the chosen fluid beneath the prepared study row. The batch API validates the same rule and issues separate `CLINICAL_LAB`, `PATHOLOGY_CYTOLOGY`, and `GENETICS_MOLECULAR` orders when appropriate. Issued items retain canonical IDs, item IDs, routing and immutable specimen snapshots. Portable HTML/PDF displays human specimen labels; one molecular panel prints as one study. Current exact-order-item result registration accepts the unstructured/PDF report. No structured organism targets, analyte components, derived calculations, provider availability claim, or actual-collection workflow was added.

Sterile-fluid culture remains a single orderable across four approved fluids. Gram staining and susceptibility, when performed, are included/reflex downstream work according to the laboratory; no separate Gram or susceptibility orderable was created.
