## Method and boundaries

The read-only Director review database `mxmed_director_review_lon07c.clinical_study_types` had **252 active identities**. We checked every active key and its category/alias against `study_featured_navigation_v1.json` (FEATURED02), HIER03, SEARCH02-R1's 252-key `study_search_authority_v1.json`, LAB-CAT04, CAT03B `study_specimen_requirements_v1.json`, Dental CAT02, and `study_order_routing_v1.json`. The 49 final leaves are 24 laboratory, 10 imaging, 2 pathology, 5 functional, 4 diagnostic-procedure, and 4 dental. The 252 keys each have a leaf; secondary appearances do not create an additional identity. Family counts below assign dental-specific imaging keys to Dental and genetics to Laboratory. The gap CSV contains **one row for every active identity (252) and 74 additional candidate concepts**. An active baseline row is not a claim that the test is common. Candidate priorities are P1 general Mexico, P2 specialty Mexico, P3 advanced/reference, P4 rare/provider-specific. P1 is deliberately conservative.

The scorecard's **COMMON_EXPECTED_CONCEPT_COUNT is the bounded curated P1/P2 comparison set in the CSV**, including representative active items and candidate concepts of all classifications. It is not an estimate of the total orderables in Mexico, a quality score, or a claim that all 252 active keys are common. TRUE_MISSING counts include both new single identities and fixed-component panels; these are proposals until the Director approves each boundary. P3/uncertain items do not enter the minimum safe set. Source evidence proves an offering or service family, not nationwide access, provider eligibility, indication, or a universal billing code. Rows with family-level evidence explicitly require exact item confirmation before implementation.

The classification is mutually exclusive per row: `EXACT_EXISTING`, `ALIAS_OF_EXISTING`, `MISSING_NAVIGATION_ROUTE`, `MISSING_SEARCH_TERM`, `TRUE_MISSING_CANONICAL`, `TRUE_MISSING_PANEL`, `PARAMETER_NOT_IDENTITY`, `RESULT_COMPONENT_NOT_ORDERABLE`, `DERIVED_VALUE`, `PROCEDURE_NOT_STUDY`, `PROVIDER_CONFIGURATION`, `ADVANCED_DEFER`, or `UNCERTAIN`. In particular, a clinician's alternative name is not evidence for a new canonical identity. SEARCH02-R1 already provides normalized prefix and common-name search; only demonstrated remaining terms (for example *Papanicolau* and *panendoscopia*) are search gaps. The CSV preserves the existing canonical key beside those terms.

## Mexico-market evidence and clinical interpretation

The audit used independent public and private Mexican sources, including [Chopo's pathology/oncology services](https://www.chopo.com.mx/morelia/estudios/oncologia), [Salud Digna's nationwide service categories](https://www.salud-digna.org/citas), [Quest México's laboratory services](https://questdiagnostics.com.mx/hospitales-y-laboratorios/), [Laboratorio Médico Polanco's hepatic panel](https://lmpolanco.com/perfil-funcion-hepatica-integral/) and [thyroid panel](https://lmpolanco.com/perfil-tiroides/), [Médica Sur pathology](https://medicasur.com.mx/es/ms/Anatomia_Patologica_2019), [HGM pathology organization](https://hgm.salud.gob.mx/normateca/manuales_de_organizacion/DCM/MAN_ORG_ANA_PAT_2025.pdf), [HGM endoscopy procedures](https://hgm.salud.gob.mx/normateca/manuales_de_procedimientos/DCM/MAN_PROC_SERV_END_2024.pdf), [IMSS cardiopulmonary guidance](https://www.imss.gob.mx/sites/all/statics/profesionalesSalud/investigacionSalud/historico/programas/12-pai-epoc.pdf), and Mexican dental/maxillofacial services in the source CSV. The [DOF anatomy-pathology standard](https://dof.gob.mx/normasOficiales/6428/salud12_C/salud12_C.html) is the official anchor for the distinction between specimen handling and pathology reporting. [LOINC's order/observation distinction](https://loinc.org/kb/users-guide/loinc-database-structure/loinc-table-structure) and [panel guidance](https://loinc.org/kb/users-guide/panels/types-of-results) inform panel boundaries; [CAP cancer protocols](https://www.cap.org/protocols-and-guidelines/cancer-protocols/) inform future structured pathology reports. [Mayo's anatomy-pathology consultation catalog](https://prd1.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/70318) illustrates material-specific specimen handling. [ACR/RadiologyInfo on body CT](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/bodyct) and [MRA](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/angiomr) support the contrast-parameter versus vascular-technique distinction. These international references do not establish Mexican availability.

Médica Sur, HGM, IMSS, and Chopo independently describe histology, surgical specimens, immunohistochemistry, immunofluorescence, or special pathology workflows. HGM's [2024 service statistics](https://hgm.salud.gob.mx/interna/dirgral/descargas/Ene-Dic_24_11-04-25.pdf) record 26,278 biopsies, 5,609 cytologies, 2,574 immunohistochemistry studies, and 309 immunofluorescence studies. These are institutional counts, **not national prevalence**. They corroborate that a five-cytology-only order catalog is materially incomplete.

## Pathology: Director decision

**Current active: 5, all cytology** (`cyto_pap`, `cyto_liquid_based`, `urine_cytology`, `csf_cytology`, `serous_fluid_cytology`). Current model has two final leaves, cervical cytology and fluid cytology. **Mexico evidence shows the catalog is incomplete: yes.** Recommend a **hybrid V1**: a small set of method/workflow-specific orderable identities, with structured specimen/site/technique parameters. An organ-by-organ SKU catalog would explode and obscure which work is being ordered; one wholly generic “patología” identity would conflate materially different handling and result timelines. Split by organ only when preparation, routing, required report, or clinical eligibility truly differs.

Proposed pathology leaves: histopathology biopsy/resection; cytology (cervical, fluid, FNA, respiratory); IHC/biomarkers; histochemistry/special stains; immunofluorescence; intraoperative/frozen; outside material review; hematopathology; and molecular pathology as a **cross-route** to the existing genetics `somatic_tumor_ngs`, not a duplicate. A leaf should be introduced only with enough supported orderables; hematopathology may initially be a group in histopathology.

**Minimum safe identities, staged together only after specimen contract review:** `histopath_biopsy`, `histopath_resection`, `cyto_fna`, `ihc_single_marker`, fixed-component `ihc_breast_profile`, `frozen_section`, then `cyto_bronchial`, `histochemical_special_stain`, `if_renal`, `if_skin`, `pathology_outside_review`. The remaining P2 proposals (`cyto_sputum`, `her2_ish`, `bone_marrow_histology`) require local provider/workflow confirmation. PAS/Grocott/Masson/Ziehl-Neelsen/reticulin should be values of a controlled stain selector under `histochemical_special_stain`, not five independent names. ER/PR/HER2/Ki67 can be markers under single-marker IHC or components in a **versioned** breast profile; biomarker observations are not automatically new orderable identities. HER2 ISH differs by technique and may warrant a separate P2 identity. Tissue NGS has an existing canonical identity in genetics and needs discoverability, not duplication.

**Mandatory biopsy boundary:** image-guided, endoscopic, and skin biopsy *acquisition* are procedural services with operator, consent, anatomy, and collection event. Histopathology of the acquired specimen is a separate pathology order/result with pathology accession and container/slide lineage. A combined provider package may orchestrate both but must not collapse the two authorities. Diagnostic/therapeutic acts remain outside any new study writer.

**CAT03B specimen gap:** current allowed specimens include fluids, blood, semen, saliva, sputum, BAL, and bronchial wash, plus limited collection/source attributes. It does not support `TISSUE`; structured organ/anatomic site and laterality; biopsy versus resection; number and ID of containers; fresh/fixed state and fixative; collection/receipt timestamps; block/slide identifiers and external origin; cytology preparation/adequacy; margin/orientation for resection; linked source procedure; or preliminary/final/addendum relationships. These are proposed contract needs, **not changes made here**. Do not send tissue through the fluid specimen contract by free text. Frozen section needs a timely preliminary result explicitly linked to definitive final histology.

**Result model:** a canonical PDF/unstructured report can safely carry routine histology, cytology, outside review, special stains, and an initial IHC interpretation if it retains exact source order/item and specimen identifiers. Future structured fields are valuable for synoptic malignancy reports, margins/staging, ER/PR/HER2/Ki67, molecular variants, IF patterns, frozen preliminary/final linkage, and quality/adequacy. Lack of synoptic structure should not block safe ordering and report attachment now; it does require a clear staged roadmap. The DOF standard governs mandatory report provenance, and CAP supplies a future structure reference.

## Imaging and parameter boundaries

Current 34 non-dental imaging identities already cover plain RX, selected contrast fluoroscopy, ultrasound/Doppler, obstetric and cardiac ultrasound (the latter mapped in functional), CT, MR, mammography/breast US, DXA, nuclear medicine, and PET/CT. Source-supported practical gaps in the curated set are hip RX, sinus CT, lumbar MR (P1) and neck CT, CTA head/neck, MRA brain, pelvic MR, and breast tomosynthesis (P2). The [Chopo CT menu](https://www.chopo.com.mx/default/estudios/tomografia), [Chopo lumbar MR orderable](https://www.chopo.com.mx/sonora/resonancia-magnetica-de-columna-lumbar), and [Médica Sur imaging](https://ftp.medicasur.com.mx/es/ms/Imagenologia) support distinct regional services. Contrast choice is usually a **parameter on the regional study**, as the Chopo lumbar MR page explicitly describes. Side, projection count, pregnancy/trimester, and protocol are parameters. CTA/MRA are distinct vascular techniques with their own preparation/interpretation, so they are not silently inferred from standard CT/MR. PET tracer is provider/protocol configuration until evidence supports a broadly orderable separate identity. BI-RADS is a result component.

Proposed navigation: regional CT/MR groups and a vascular-imaging group only when supported by several studies, breast tomo under breast imaging. Preserve existing chest/head/abdomen/uro and MRI brain/joint routes. Confirm the actual provider can perform the exact region/protocol before activation; an offer on a national site is not a promise from every branch.

## Functional, procedures, laboratory, dental

**Functional:** Existing ECG, Holter, MAPA, stress/tilt/ABI, EEG/video EEG/EMG/evoked, spirometry/PFT/DLCO/plethysmography, sleep studies, audiometry/OAE/tympanometry/VNG cover the requested major modalities. `echo_tes` already represents transesophageal echocardiography; do not duplicate it. Bronchodilator response is a spirometry protocol parameter. BAEP (`evoked_auditory_baep`) is present under neurophysiology and may need an audiology discovery route. Event monitors and ambulatory EEG remain uncertain pending exact Mexican provider menus. The minimum safe functional phase is **zero new identities**, plus those parameter/navigation checks.

**Diagnostic procedures:** Existing EGD, colonoscopy, sigmoidoscopy, enteroscopy, ERCP, EUS, bronchoscopy, EBUS, laryngoscopy and pleuroscopy are real entries. *Panendoscopia* is a search term for EGD, not a second procedure. [HGM's endoscopy manual](https://hgm.salud.gob.mx/normateca/manuales_de_procedimientos/DCM/MAN_PROC_SERV_END_2024.pdf) separates diagnostic and therapeutic workflows. The minimum safe gap is colposcopy, supported by [HGM's colposcopy clinic](https://hgm.salud.gob.mx/interna/unidades/onco/onco.html) and [Polanco's service brochure](https://lmpolanco.com/pdf/CV_LaboratorioM%C3%A9dicoPolanco.pdf). Esophageal manometry and pH/impedance are P2; before implementation the Director should decide whether these sit under functional studies or diagnostic procedures and establish a specific routing group. Polypectomy, stone extraction during ERCP, and acquisition biopsies are procedures/therapy, not catalog study synonyms. Do not activate new procedure writers in this audit.

**Laboratory:** LAB-CAT04 materially expanded the catalog to 152 identities including genetics. The visible chemistry/hepatic/thyroid leaf groupings **are navigation groups**, not fixed orderable panels. [Salud Digna lists Q3 and Q6 as distinct packages](https://empresas.salud-digna.org/afiliados); [Polanco lists hepatic](https://lmpolanco.com/perfil-funcion-hepatica-integral/) and [thyroid profiles](https://lmpolanco.com/perfil-tiroides/). The minimum safe proposal is Q3, Q6, hepatic, and thyroid fixed-component/versioned panels, plus arterial blood gas with arterial specimen and inspired oxygen/collection context. P2 IGRA and AMH are supported in specialty laboratory menus but need exact provider confirmation. Do not turn an arbitrary commercial bundle into a canonical panel without fixed composition. Anion gap and eGFR are derived results, not orders; FIT versus current `fecal_occult_blood` is **UNCERTAIN** until the existing assay method is inspected. LOINC's panel/observation boundary supports these decisions.

**Dental:** Current 7 identities include panoramic, lateral cephalometry, CBCT, TMJ comparative RX, photos, intraoral scanning and models. [Centro de Radiología Maxilofacial](https://www.centroderadiologiamaxilofacial.com.mx/), [Dentalis](https://dentalis.mx/servicios/), [IDIMAX](https://idimax.com.mx/estudios/), and [IMSS dental guidance](https://www.imss.gob.mx/sites/all/statics/pdf/procedimientos/2250-003-002.pdf) support the missing intraoral cluster. Minimum safe: periapical and bitewing identities (P1); occlusal RX and a versioned full periapical series (P2). Keep FDI tooth/quadrant selection, view count, arch and CBCT field of view as structured parameters. An orthodontic records *package* references individual studies rather than becoming one universal clinical identity.

## New subfamilies and phase order

Proposed clusters, subject to Director approval: pathology histopathology, IHC/biomarkers, special stains, IF, intraoperative, outside review; imaging regional CT/MR and vascular imaging; diagnostic procedures colposcopy and esophageal motility (root decision pending); laboratory fixed clinical profiles and blood gases; dental intraoral radiography. Do not create a leaf for one obscure P3 item. Search and cross-routes should be changed only after each clinical identity/parameter boundary is approved.

1. **PATH-CAT01** — highest material gap. First approve tissue specimen/result boundaries, then 6 P1 + up to 8 P2 identities/panels (14 maximum); no biopsy acquisition writer. Preserve exact source order/item linkage.
2. **IMG-CAT01** — approve regional versus contrast/vascular parameters, then 3 P1 + 5 P2 identities (8 maximum) and cluster routes.
3. **LAB-CAT05** — approve versioned panel compositions and arterial sample contract, then 5 P1 + 2 P2 identities/panels (7 maximum). Resolve FIT ambiguity separately.
4. **DENTAL-CAT03** — preserve FDI and add 2 P1 + 2 P2 orderables/panels (4 maximum).
5. **PROC-CAT01** — decide procedural authority/routing and consent separation before up to 3 diagnostic entries; no therapeutic writer.
6. **FUNC-CAT01** — parameter/search/navigation follow-up; zero new canonical identities approved by this audit. Reopen uncertain prolonged studies only with direct Mexican service evidence.

This sequence follows magnitude/product impact while keeping each future canonical addition well below ~20–30 per phase. All phase labels remain proposals; none authorizes implementation.
