## Evidence and current authority

The live search authority is `modules/clinical/catalog/study_search_authority_v1.json` (287 active entries), the HIER03/FEATURED02 leaf authority is `study_featured_navigation_v1.json`, and the routing authority is `study_order_routing_v1.json`. The implementation matrix enumerates **all 41** keys, their existing names and routes, and the disposition of 19 candidate concepts. The source matrix records independent Mexican primary evidence across cardiology, neurophysiology, pulmonology, sleep, audiology and GI physiology. A service list documents availability, not prevalence; P2 is a targeted specialty need rather than a national volume estimate.

The historical 41 count must not be presented as 41 visible Functional Studies. `carotid_doppler`, `lower_ext_art_doppler`, `lower_ext_venous_doppler`, `echo_tes`, `echo_tte` and `stress_echo` are the six imaging studies. They reside in `ultrasound_vascular` or `ultrasound_cardiac` under Imaging, although their operational route is `CARDIOVASCULAR_DIAGNOSTICS`. This is correct; no duplicate functional catalog row or navigation move is proposed. Stress echo is distinct from exercise ECG, and nuclear myocardial perfusion is another imaging identity.

Functional Studies measures physiology. Imaging acquires physical images; diagnostic procedures perform endoscopic or other invasive examinations; laboratory tests analyze specimens; pathology interprets tissue/cells; therapeutic procedures treat. GI manometry and pH monitoring are physiologic measurements even when an endoscopy service delivers them. The [IMSS specialty division](https://edumed.imss.gob.mx/pediatria/nueshosp/divespmed/pagdivespmed.htm) names these tests in digestive physiology; an [IMSS procurement document](https://reposipot.imss.gob.mx/OOAD/COLIMA/2025/1.%20PRIMER%20TRIMESTRE%202025/LA-50-GYR-050GYR012-N-15-2025/2.-%20CONVOC%20LA-012-N-15-25.pdf) lists manometry and pH testing as separate services. This supports two P2 identities, not one combined order.

## Current domain decisions

### Cardiovascular

Keep one `ecg_12lead` identity for a routine 12-lead ECG; `ecg_rhythm_strip` remains a narrower recording. Add qualified EKG/electrocardiografía discovery, without creating abbreviation rows. Keep `holter` and `abpm_mapa` separate: the former monitors ECG, the latter blood pressure. “Holter de presión” may point to MAPA only as a **qualified** phrase; bare “Holter” must stay with ECG monitoring. Holter 24/48/72-hour duration is an order parameter and exact provider capability. Extended event-triggered monitors remain `UNCERTAIN`, since they may require different devices and clinical intent. Keep `stress_test` for exercise ECG/ergometry/caminadora, with protocol as a parameter; never map it to stress echo or myocardial perfusion. Keep `ankle_brachial_index` and `tilt_table`; both already exist. [IMSS procurement](https://reposipot.imss.gob.mx/OOAD/COLIMA/2025/3.%20TERCER%20TRIMESTRE%202025/SIPOT%20LA-N-70-25/4.%20PP-012-N-70-25.pdf) distinguishes ECG, ambulatory monitors, exercise testing and tilt from echo services.

### Neurophysiology

Keep routine EEG, sleep-deprived EEG and video EEG as existing identities. The named sleep-deprivation workflow is already a separate catalog identity; monitoring duration and inpatient/ambulatory setting should not spawn 24/48/72-hour variants. The [IMSS description of video EEG](https://www.imss.gob.mx/prensa/archivo/202307/348) includes monitoring up to 72 hours. “EEG ambulatorio” remains `UNCERTAIN` pending evidence that it is a distinct service, rather than a setting/protocol of video EEG. `emg_ncs` represents the combined EMG and nerve-conduction request; add common electrodiagnostic terms, and require body site/side/requested nerve or muscle scope so a partial examination is not silently broadened. Preserve separate visual, somatosensory and auditory evoked potential identities. BAEP/PEATC remains routed to `NEUROPHYSIOLOGY`, with cross-discovery from audiology; [IMSS research](https://revistamedica.imss.gob.mx/index.php/revista_medica/article/view/297) discusses it alongside audiometry. A provider must offer the exact BAEP key, regardless of department label.

### Pulmonary and sleep

Keep `spirometry` as one identity and represent baseline vs pre/post bronchodilator as a structured protocol choice. `full_pft`, `dlco` and `plethysmography` remain distinct capability keys. `PFT` is currently an equivalent alias of **both** `spirometry` and `full_pft`; disambiguate it to the full test after search regression QA. Preserve six-minute walk, CPET, FeNO, capnography and overnight oximetry. [INER spirometry guidance](https://www.iner.salud.gob.mx/descargas/dirmedica/fisiologiarespiratoria/guiastecnicas/2-Espirometria.pdf) and its [functional-test review](https://iner.salud.gob.mx/descargas/dirmedica/fisiologiarespiratoria/guiastecnicas/1-PFRCual_Quien.pdf) support a protocol distinction and separate lung-volume/diffusion capabilities.

Keep `psg_diagnostic`, `hsat`, `psg_titration`, `mslt` and `mwt` separate. Overnight oximetry is under pulmonary navigation and cannot imply full PSG/HSAT. PAP titration as a sleep study differs from later CPAP/BPAP therapeutic setup. The [IMSS sleep overview](https://www.imss.gob.mx/node/103473) distinguishes home and laboratory testing; the [INER sleep service manual](https://iner.salud.gob.mx/descargas/normatecainterna/MPdirmedica/MP_SERVMEDICINASUENO_28102025.pdf) describes PSG delivery and reporting.

### Audiology and vestibular testing

Keep tonal and speech audiometry, tympanometry, otoacoustic emissions and VNG as five separate exact offerings. Add “logoaudiometría” for speech audiometry. Tympanometry already has “impedanciometría”. Caloric testing can be an explicit VNG protocol/component when available; do not claim every VNG offering includes it. vHIT and dynamic posturography are **not** aliases of VNG and should be `ADVANCED_DEFER` until specialty demand, provider capability and intended ordering scope are demonstrated. The [Mexican audiology norm hosted by IMSS](https://www.imss.gob.mx/sites/all/statics/pdf/marconormativo/NormasOficiales/4366.pdf) lists the ordinary tests distinctly. BAEP can appear in audiology search, while its existing neurophysiology route remains authoritative.

### Gastrointestinal motility

Approve **in principle**, subject to a contract and Director review, `esophageal_manometry` (display “Manometría esofágica”) and `esophageal_ph_monitoring` (display “Monitoreo de pH esofágico”). High-resolution versus conventional manometry is a technique/capability, not a default extra identity. For pH monitoring, distinguish pH-only from combined pH-impedance in structured technique and exact provider offering; a pH-only provider must not match an impedance-pH order by route alone. Capture requested duration and on/off acid suppression, without encoding the default 24 hours into the identity. A new Functional Studies leaf “Motilidad gastrointestinal” is warranted by **two** independently orderable studies. A dedicated `FUNCTIONAL_GI_PHYSIOLOGY` route is justified; `DIGESTIVE_ENDOSCOPY` is a different service. Category vocabulary/DB constraints and route enum need review in the implementation phase. [IMSS tender evidence](https://reposipot.imss.gob.mx/OOAD/NAYARIT/4to%20TRIMESTRE%202025/4TO.%20TRIMESTRE/DICIEMBRE/050GYR005N26125-001-00.pdf) distinguishes esophageal, pH-only, pH-impedance and anorectal services. Anorectal manometry belongs to the same future functional leaf conceptually but is **ADVANCED_DEFER**: evidence of specialized provision does not establish a general P2 need. No anorectal activation is authorized here.

## Proposed parameter authority and downstream boundaries

A bounded, versioned `functional_order_parameters_v1` is justified because **multiple current** studies have clinically material choices: Holter/MAPA/video EEG recording duration, spirometry bronchodilator intent, EMG/evoked/audiology laterality and body site, stress-test protocol, VNG caloric intent, and PSG titration protocol. This is not a free-form bag. It should specify per-key allowlists, typed ranges/enums, required-versus-optional rules, exact provider-capability checks where a variant changes equipment, and an immutable human-readable order snapshot. Duration is a shared measured quantity but not a shared assumption: each study needs permitted ranges and units. For GI, manometry technique and pH-only versus pH-impedance are required choices before activation. No null/default must silently mean that a provider can perform all variants.

The current write normalization in `api/_lib/clinical_study_contract.php` handles pathology and imaging structured parameters, but no functional-specific parameter. Composition accepts those existing fields and generic notes; portable-order read/print validates pathology and imaging labels, not a functional label. The next contract task must update all three together, preserving exact order-version snapshots, and print clinically necessary duration, site/side, protocol and technique on portable HTML/PDF. This is a proposed future implementation, not a claim that the current UI already captures these fields.

Current result linkage is based on exact source-order version and `related_order_item_ids`; that is adequate for attached ECG waveform/report, Holter, EEG, PFT, sleep and audiology files or reports. Native waveform integration is outside catalog completeness. An order's physiology parameters must remain in its snapshot; a result links to the exact source item, and a lineage-head display must not infer successor coverage. Provider matching uses the exact `study_type_id` offering, as shown by `healthcare_study_interop.php`; a routing group alone cannot imply ECG→Holter, Holter→MAPA, EEG→video EEG, spirometry→full PFT or audiometry→BAEP capability. New protocol variants must also be checked against explicit provider capability before automatic match. No existing route is changed by this audit.
