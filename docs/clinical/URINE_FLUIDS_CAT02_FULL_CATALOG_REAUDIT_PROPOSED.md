## Findings by clinical area

### Urine: general, renal, electrolytes and lithiasis

Keep EGO, microalbumin, spot ACR, spot PCR and osmolality distinct. Do not reinterpret `microalbumin` as including creatinine. EGO's pH, specific gravity, glucose, ketones, blood, nitrite, leukocyte esterase and microscopy are result components **within EGO**. Separate source order definitions do exist for random urine pH, specific gravity and free hemoglobin; they are recorded as conditional candidates, not automatically dismissed as EGO duplicates or promoted to the safe shortlist. Mayo explicitly identifies random urine total protein as non-orderable alone in one workflow, while the spot protein/creatinine profile is orderable; this audit therefore **removed spot total protein from the safe shortlist**. [Mayo EGO/urinalysis](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/urinalysis/about/pac-20384907), [random pH](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/606510), [specific gravity](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/606565), [Labcorp free hemoglobin](https://www.labcorp.com/tests/080176/hemoglobin-hb-free-qualitative-urine), [random protein component](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/617782/protein-total-random-urine-non-orderable), [spot PCR](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/614004).

Spot urinary creatinine, sodium and potassium have individually orderable source definitions; these may be fixed-specimen CAT03A identities. A serum order must never be reused for urine. Other electrolytes/minerals (chloride, calcium, phosphate, magnesium, uric acid, ammonium, urea nitrogen, sulfate) occur in timed urine testing and/or stone profiles; audit each standalone offering before activation. The [Mayo 24-hour supersaturation profile](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/616180) explicitly labels many components available separately and others not; it also requires duration and volume. [Random sodium](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/610785), [random potassium](https://prd1.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/610696), [random creatinine](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/610603).

Model 12-hour and 24-hour collections as a defined analyte order plus collection protocol; do not generate a canonical key for every duration. A 2-hour protein collection remains **uncertain** until a source/local offering is verified. Stone-risk/supersaturation profiles are genuine source panels, while supersaturation indices and estimated protein catabolic rate are derived outputs. Random vs 24-hour stone profiles have different collection requirements and potentially different composition. No universal stone panel should be activated until versioned composition and timing rules are specified. [Mayo random stone profile](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/616375), [Mayo 24-hour profile](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/616180).

### Urine: endocrine, immunology and microbiology

Free urine cortisol, metanephrines, catecholamines and 5-HIAA are real reference-lab orderables, usually source-defined timed collections. Keep their **primary route** endocrine/metabolic rather than moving them to the fluid leaf solely because the specimen is urine. VMA, urinary aldosterone, copper and iodine were not established strongly enough by this reviewed source set and remain uncertain. Porphyrin/PBG and urinary monoclonal protein studies are specialty orders/panels; paired and timed details matter. [Mayo free cortisol](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/8546), [metanephrines](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/83006), [catecholamines](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/9276), [5-HIAA](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/9248), [urine monoclonal study](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/616911).

Keep `urine_culture` as the culture authority. Susceptibility can be a reflex/result of that workflow and should not become a second routine physician CTA. A separate Gram order, CT/NG urine NAAT and any UTI molecular panel need their own organism/method/source definition; none is an alias of culture. [Mayo urine culture and susceptibility](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/60515), [CT/NG NAAT](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/61553).

### CSF / LCR

Keep `csf_cell_count` for count and differential, with differential fractions as components. Source-supported distinct orders include CSF glucose and total protein (the CAT03A shortlist), lactate, Gram/culture, cryptococcal antigen, oligoclonal bands, IgG index and meningitis/encephalitis PCR. Paired serum is essential to the oligoclonal/IgG-index model; CSF IgG and albumin values and their quotient are profile components/derived results here, not three automatic new orders. Cytology and flow cytometry belong primarily to pathology/hematopathology, not to cell count. Independent CSF chloride orderability remains unverified. [Mayo CSF glucose](https://prd1.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/152), [protein](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/872), [oligoclonal profile](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/8017), [IgG-index profile](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/610783), [molecular panel](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/622764), [Michigan CSF cytology](https://mlabs.umich.edu/tests/cerebrospinal-fluid-cytology).

### Pleural, ascitic/peritoneal, synovial and pericardial fluid

Use a **small set of source-aware body-fluid analyte identities** for glucose, total protein, albumin, LDH, amylase, triglycerides and cholesterol, plus a body-fluid cell-count/differential identity, subject to each test's validated fluid list. Mayo's body-fluid cell-count test accepts synovial, pleural, peritoneal and pericardial sources under one order; its chemistry pages similarly require a fluid source. This supports model **C for V1** (a small set of explicit body-fluid orderables), evolving toward **B** (analyte + structured validated specimen parameter), and argues against **A** (one key per fluid × analyte). The current MXMED model supports neither B nor safe broad body-fluid C yet. [Mayo BFCC](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/608873), [body-fluid glucose](https://prd1.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/606609), [protein](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/606619), [albumin](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/800507), [LDH](https://prd1.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/606612), [amylase](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/606595), [triglycerides](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/800539), [cholesterol](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/606915).

Pleural pH needs a local blood-gas style handling path; Mayo's generic body-fluid pH definition specifically excludes pleural pH. Light's criteria require paired serum and pleural measurements and are derived, as is SAAG from serum and ascitic albumin. Do not seed either as a standalone order. Pericardial LDH is deliberately **uncertain/low priority**, because Mayo notes limited diagnostic utility. The existing `synovial_crystals` covers urate and CPPD crystal findings; do not create separate crystal orders. Gram, culture, AFB and cytology require source-aware microbiology or pathology authority, rather than one duplicate study per cavity. [Mayo body-fluid pH](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/606522), [Michigan sterile-fluid culture](https://mlabs.umich.edu/tests/aerobic-culture-sterile-body-fluid), [Michigan crystal exam](https://mlabs.umich.edu/tests/body-fluid-analysis-crystal-exam).

### Semen and other specimens

`semen_analysis` is the basic fertility order. Concentration, motility, volume, basic morphology, vitality and leukocytes are primarily report components, not six new orders. A post-vasectomy check is a **separate** source order and a CAT03A candidate. Strict morphology, antisperm antibody studies and culture require separate validated definitions, not automatic derivation from the basic order. Saliva, sputum, BAL, bronchial washings, tracheal aspirates, drains, cyst fluid, gastric aspirate and wound/body-cavity fluid should route chiefly by endocrine, microbiology, pathology, molecular or chemistry intent; specimen and site are parameters. [Mayo semen analysis](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/81641), [post-vasectomy check](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/9205), [fertility catalog](https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/fertility-testing), [salivary cortisol](https://prd1.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/84225).

## Panels, navigation and featured review

**True source panels/profiles:** existing ACR and PCR; proposed but deferred random/24-hour stone supersaturation, CSF meningitis/encephalitis PCR, paired CSF oligoclonal/IgG studies, urinary monoclonal-protein profile, and body-fluid lipid analysis. **Physician presets are not canonical panels:** an arbitrary clinician bundle of EGO + culture + ACR, a cavity workup assembled from chemistry + culture + cytology, or a convenience group of timed urine analytes. A true panel requires a source test identity and fixed/versioned composition or a precisely defined provider order.

Keep the six current featured keys and their order for now: `urinalysis` **KEEP**, `microalbumin` **KEEP**, `urine_culture` **KEEP**, `urine_albumin_creatinine_panel` **KEEP**, `urine_protein_creatinine_panel` **KEEP**, `urine_osmolality` **KEEP**. They are all existing, have clear fixed scope, and fit the six-item cap. Revisit ordering only with local utilization data. The new CSF/semen candidates should not displace common urine studies solely for cross-fluid coverage.

Future full-catalog accordion proposal, **without changing HIER03**: keep “Generales y renales”, “Microbiología urinaria”, “LCR”, “Líquido sinovial” and “Semen”; add “Electrolitos y minerales” and “Litiasis y metabolismo urinario” only after active canonical rows exist; consider “Líquido pleural / ascítico / pericárdico” as one source-aware body-fluid chemistry group, not three empty or duplicative groups. Specialized endocrine, molecular, pathology and microbiology orders should retain their primary clinical-family navigation, with optional fluid-based discovery links. Do not create empty accordions.
